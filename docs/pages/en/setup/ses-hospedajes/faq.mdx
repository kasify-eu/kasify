Common questions from hosts about the SES Hospedajes guest registration obligation and how Kasify handles it.

## Do I need to register friends and family staying at my house for free?

No. SES Hospedajes only requires guest registration when the stay involves payment or some other form of compensation. If family or friends stay free of charge, with no money or other consideration involved, there is no obligation to report the stay.
