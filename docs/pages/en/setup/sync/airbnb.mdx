---
title: Sync your Airbnb calendar
navigation_label: Connect Airbnb
---

Connect your Airbnb listing to Kasify so bookings are imported into your calendar **automatically**. You don't have to type anything in: as soon as a guest books on Airbnb, the stay shows up in Kasify.

The connection is set up **per property** and works **one way**: Kasify reads the Airbnb calendar, but it doesn't change your listing.

## 1. Copy the calendar link from Airbnb

Airbnb provides a calendar link (*iCal* format) for each listing:

1. Sign in to Airbnb and open the **Calendar** for the listing you want to connect.
2. Find the **Connect calendars** section and click **Connect to another website**.
3. In the panel that opens, under **Step 1 — Add this link to the other website**, click **Copy** next to **Airbnb calendar link**.

The link starts with `https://www.airbnb.com/…`.

> Copy the **Airbnb calendar link** from that panel, not the address in your browser's bar. They're different.

:::info
For Airbnb's own step-by-step guide, see [Sync your calendar to other websites](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/99) in the Airbnb Help Center.
:::

## 2. Paste the link into Kasify

1. In Kasify, open **Properties** and go into the relevant property.
2. Click **Settings**.
3. Go to the **Airbnb Integration** section.
4. Paste the URL into the **Airbnb Calendar Link** field.
5. Click **Save Changes**.

You can also do this when you first add the property, from **Add Property**: the **Airbnb Integration** section is in the same form.

## When bookings appear

The first sync can take **a few minutes**. After that, Kasify re-checks the Airbnb calendar **automatically on a regular basis** (roughly every hour), with nothing needed from you.

## Tips and troubleshooting

- **If no bookings appear**, check that you pasted the correct export link. An incorrect URL is flagged as an error and imports nothing (rather than silently syncing zero bookings).
- **Each property has its own link.** If you manage several listings, repeat the steps on each property using that listing's link.
- **To disconnect Airbnb**, clear the URL with the "x" (**Clear URL**) next to the field and click **Save Changes**. New bookings from that channel will stop importing.
- **Import only.** Kasify doesn't send your calendar back to Airbnb; to block dates on Airbnb, use Airbnb's own tools.
