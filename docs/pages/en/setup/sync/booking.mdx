---
title: Sync your Booking.com calendar
navigation_label: Connect Booking.com
---

Connect your Booking.com listing to Kasify so bookings are imported into your calendar **automatically**. As soon as a reservation comes in on Booking.com, the booked dates show up in Kasify.

The connection is set up **per property** and works **one way**: Kasify reads the Booking.com calendar, but it doesn't change your listing.

## 1. Copy the calendar link from Booking.com

Booking.com provides an export link (*iCal* format) for each listing from its extranet:

1. Sign in to the **Booking.com extranet** and select the property you want to connect.
2. Open **Rates & Availability** and go to **Sync calendars (iCal)**.
3. In the export section, copy the calendar URL. It starts with `https://ical.booking.com/v1/export?t=…`.

> Copy the **export** link that Booking.com gives you, not the address in your browser's bar. They're different.

:::info
For Booking.com's own step-by-step guide, see [How to synchronize your calendars across channels](https://partner.booking.com/en-us/help/rates-availability/extranet-calendar/how-synchronize-your-calendars-across-channels) in the Booking.com Partner Help Center.
:::

## 2. Paste the link into Kasify

1. In Kasify, open **Properties** and go into the relevant property.
2. Click **Settings**.
3. Go to the **Booking.com Integration** section.
4. Paste the URL into the **Booking.com Calendar Link** field.
5. Click **Save Changes**.

You can also do this when you first add the property, from **Add Property**: the **Booking.com Integration** section is in the same form.

## When bookings appear

The first sync can take **a few minutes**. After that, Kasify re-checks the Booking.com calendar **automatically on a regular basis** (roughly every hour), with nothing needed from you.

## Tips and troubleshooting

- **Guest details.** Booking.com exports its reservations as "Not available" date blocks. Because of that, Kasify imports the **booked dates** from this channel, but not guest details through the calendar.
- **If no bookings appear**, check that you pasted the correct export link. An incorrect URL is flagged as an error and imports nothing (rather than silently syncing zero bookings).
- **Each property has its own link.** If you manage several listings, repeat the steps on each property using that listing's link.
- **To disconnect Booking.com**, clear the URL with the "x" (**Clear URL**) next to the field and click **Save Changes**. New bookings from that channel will stop importing.
- **Import only.** Kasify doesn't send your calendar back to Booking.com; to block dates on Booking.com, use its extranet tools.
