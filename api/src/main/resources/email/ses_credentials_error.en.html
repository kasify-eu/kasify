  how to fix it  
1. The web-service credentials are not your SES portal (Cl@ve) login. They are created in the SES portal under “Envío de comunicaciones por servicio web” (the WS username usually ends in _WS).
2. Copy the landlord code (codigoArrendador) exactly as shown in the portal — it is 10 characters and leading zeros matter.
3. Landlord code, WS username and WS password must all belong to the same SES registration.
