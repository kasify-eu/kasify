SES Hospedajes rejected your credentials

The Spanish guest registry (SES Hospedajes) rejected a submission from Kasify
because it did not accept your web-service credentials.

--------------------------

Affected configuration
--------------------------
WS username:   {{ses_username}}
Landlord code: {{arrendador_code}}

Guest-registry submissions that rely on these credentials are PAUSED until
they are fixed. Once you update them in Kasify, pending submissions resume
automatically within the hour.

How to fix it
--------------------------
1. The web-service credentials are NOT your SES portal (Cl@ve) login. They are
   created in the SES portal under "Envío de comunicaciones por servicio web"
   (the WS username usually ends in _WS).
2. Copy the landlord code (codigoArrendador) exactly as shown in the portal —
   it is 10 characters and leading zeros matter.
3. Landlord code, WS username and WS password must all belong to the same SES
   registration.
4. Enter the updated credentials in Kasify: {{settings_url}}
